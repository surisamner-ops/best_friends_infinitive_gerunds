Answer these questions before reading the dialogue

💬 Task 2: Read the Dialogue

Choose the correct form: gerund (-ing), infinitive (to + verb), or bare infinitive.

📖 Task 3: Comprehension Questions

Answer the questions about the dialogue

1. Why is Emma worried about the school project?
2. What character qualities does Jake mention about Emma?
3. What do they decide to do together?

✍️ Task 4: Create Your Own Dialogue

Write a dialogue (6 statements and 6 responses) about BEST FRIENDS. Use at least 4 CHARACTER QUALITIES words.

Requirements:

  • Topic: Best Friends
  • Length: 6 statements + 6 responses (12 lines total)
